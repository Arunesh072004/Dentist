## B. Client Verification Required
- *Patient Numbers:* "15,000+ patients treated"

## C. Clinical Review Required
- *All-on-X Stability Claims:* Assertions regarding bone density requirements for All-on-4 vs All-on-6.
- *Immediate Loading:* Claims regarding "teeth in a day" or same-day functional loading.
- *Graftless Protocols:* Blanket claims that Zygomatic or All-on-4 completely eliminate the need for grafting.
- *Cosmetic Outcomes:* Guaranteed shade improvements for teeth whitening.

- *Success Rates:* E.g., "99% implant success rate"
- *Guarantees:* E.g., "Lifetime warranty on implants"
- *Absolute Superlatives:* "Best dental clinic in Delhi", "No. 1 implantologist"
