Clinical Concept & Structural Segmentation

The All-on-8 protocol is indicated for patients who possess excellent bone height and density throughout the anterior and posterior alveolar ridges. Rather than relying on a single one-piece continuous horseshoe bridge, eight implants allow the prosthodontist to divide the restoration into three or four separate anatomical bridge segments (e.g., anterior 6-unit bridge and bilateral posterior 3-unit bridges).

This segmented approach mirrors natural tooth root spacing, allows natural physiological flexure of the mandibular jaw during chewing, and simplifies future prosthetic maintenance should an individual unit ever require service.

Candidate Indications

  • Patients with robust native bone width and height across the entire dental arch
  • Patients desiring segmented bridges rather than a single full-arch cross-arch splint
  • Heavy bruxers requiring maximum surface contact and stress dispersion
  • Favorable inter-arch space allowing natural emergence profiles without artificial acrylic gums

The Treatment Pathway

1. Comprehensive Ridge Mapping & Site Allocation

High-precision clinical diagnostic imaging verifies bone density in all 8 planned osteotomy sites, safeguarding vital nerves and sinuses.

2. Eight-Implant Placement

Eight titanium fixtures are systematically placed across the anterior and posterior ridge with strict torque management.

3. Healing & Osseointegration Period

Implants undergo undisturbed healing to ensure rigid osseointegration into dense trabecular bone before heavy segmented occlusal loading.

4. Segmented Zirconia Crown & Bridge Seating

Precision computer-milled individual and segmented zirconia bridge restorations are seated and occlusally balanced under clinical verification.
