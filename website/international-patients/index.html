Structured Protocol

Your Treatment Journey from Abroad

We streamline each phase of your dental travel—from initial remote inquiry through clinical execution and long-term restorative guidance.

01

Remote Review

Submit existing dental records, photographs, or x-rays for preliminary evaluation and consultation on viable treatment pathways.

02

Timeline Estimate

Receive an estimated clinical itinerary detailing necessary appointment days, surgical stages, and required healing intervals in Delhi.

03

Clinical Execution

Comprehensive on-site diagnostics, surgical placement, and prosthodontic fabrication carried out in our East Delhi facility.

04

Occlusal Verification & Fit

Precision verification of bite dynamics, prosthetic seating, aesthetics, and phonetics before departure clearance.

05

Aftercare & Documentation

Full clinical records, component passport details, and maintenance guidelines provided for continuation with your home dentist.
