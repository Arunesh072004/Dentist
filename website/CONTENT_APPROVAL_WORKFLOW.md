# CONTENT APPROVAL WORKFLOW

To ensure no unverified medical or marketing claims enter production, all new or recovered legacy content must follow this strict pipeline:

## 1. SOURCE
- Identify content from legacy domains or new marketing briefs.
- Preserve traceability to original URL if legacy.

## 2. DRAFT
- Adapt content to the new Information Architecture (IA) and Patient-Question-Driven narrative.
- Insert into the UI wireframe within `[CONTENT REQUIRED]` bounds.

## 3. CLINICAL REVIEW
- **Required for:** Indications, treatment steps, risks, recovery times, technology claims, immediate loading guarantees.
- **Action:** A verified doctor must review and sign off on the medical accuracy. 

## 4. CLIENT VERIFICATION
- **Required for:** Pricing, logistics, contact info, patient statistics, credentials, awards.
- **Action:** Clinic management must review and sign off on the factual/business accuracy.

## 5. FINAL APPROVAL
- Both Clinical and Client checks are cleared.
- Asset (if required) is cleared for usage rights and patient consent.

## 6. PUBLISHABLE
- The `[REQUIRED]` placeholders are stripped.
- The content is locked for production deployment.
