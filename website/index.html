Practice Foundations

Evidence-Guided Clinical Approach

Derived from our practice protocols, every treatment plan coordinates thorough diagnostic imaging with long-term biological maintenance.

01 · Diagnostic Protocol

Imaging & Evaluation First

Comprehensive clinical examination and radiographic assessment (including panoramic and OPG diagnostics) are conducted prior to any restorative or surgical intervention.

02 · Interdisciplinary Care

Multi-Disciplinary Coordination

Complex cases receive collaborative evaluation across surgical implantology, precision orthodontics, and restorative disciplines to ensure balanced function and aesthetics.

03 · Biological Preservation

Tissue & Bone Longevity

Our restorative philosophies prioritize natural tooth preservation where viable, alongside bone-conserving implant placement and guided regenerative therapies.

04 · Transparent Consultation

Personalized Case Planning

Patients receive direct, unhurried consultations outlining realistic clinical pathways, procedural stages, and maintenance responsibilities without commercial pressure.

Patient Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Common procedural and logistical questions regarding consultations, international visits, and initial appointments.

Where is Delhi Dental Center located?

Delhi Dental Center is located in New Delhi, India, serving patients from across Delhi NCR and international visitors. Detailed directions and appointment coordination are provided upon scheduling.

How do I schedule an initial consultation or treatment evaluation?

You can request a consultation using the appointment form on our Contact page or by submitting an inquiry. Our clinical team reviews treatment needs and coordinates diagnostic imaging appointments.

Does the clinic treat visiting international patients?

Yes. We coordinate care for international patients seeking full-mouth dental implants, restorative procedures, and orthodontic treatments, including preliminary digital review of existing radiographs before travel.

What should I bring or prepare for my first appointment?

Please bring any prior dental records, recent panoramic (OPG) radiographs if available, a list of current medications or health conditions, and any specific aesthetic or functional questions you wish to discuss.
