# CLIENT / CLINICAL HANDOFF PACKAGE

**Purpose:** This document outlines all required verifications, approvals, and assets needed from the clinic to transition the website from a structural prototype to a launch-ready production build.

## A. CLINICAL APPROVAL (Mandatory for Launch)
- **What is needed:** Exact clinical indications differentiating All-on-4, 6, and 8.
- **Pages:** `/dental-implants/all-on-4-dental-implants/`, `/dental-implants/all-on-6-dental-implants/`, `/dental-implants/all-on-8-dental-implants/`
- **Why:** To populate the Treatment Comparison Matrix accurately.
- **Status:** PENDING

## B. DOCTOR / CREDENTIAL VERIFICATION (Mandatory for Launch)
- **What is needed:** Exact names, titles, verified qualifications, and affiliations for the clinical reviewers.
- **Pages:** `/about/`, All Surgical Detail Pages
- **Why:** To establish trust and comply with the "Specialist Reviewer Block" architecture.
- **Status:** PENDING

## C. PRICING (Optional for Launch)
- **What is needed:** Accurate starting prices for implant packages and cosmetic procedures, OR confirmation to omit pricing.
- **Pages:** Quick Facts blocks across the site.
- **Why:** To prevent misleading legacy pricing from remaining live.
- **Status:** PENDING

## D. INTERNATIONAL SERVICES (Mandatory if advertising tourism)
- **What is needed:** Confirmation of explicitly provided services (Visa letters, Airport pickup, Hotel deals).
- **Pages:** `/international-patients/`
- **Why:** To avoid promising logistical support the clinic does not actually provide.
- **Status:** PENDING

## E. CONTACT INFORMATION (Mandatory for Launch)
- **What is needed:** Final phone numbers, WhatsApp, Email, Address, and Hours.
- **Pages:** Global Footer, `/contact/`, Sticky Sidebar CTAs
- **Why:** Essential for patient conversion.
- **Status:** PENDING

## F. PATIENT CASES / TESTIMONIALS (Optional, but highly recommended)
- **What is needed:** High-quality, consent-approved Before/After imagery with clinical notes.
- **Pages:** `/patient-cases/`, `/cosmetic-dentistry/teeth-whitening/`
- **Why:** To provide authentic evidence of clinical competence. No stock photos are permitted.
- **Status:** PENDING

## G. PHOTOGRAPHY / ASSETS (Mandatory for Launch)
- **What is needed:** Professional exterior/interior clinic photos, doctor portraits, medical diagrams (e.g., Zygomatic anatomy).
- **Pages:** Homepage Hero, All-on-X pages, Zygomatic page.
- **Why:** The premium editorial design system relies on high-quality real imagery.
- **Status:** PENDING
