## 2. Clinical Language Safeguards Implemented

To protect patient safety and prevent misleading medical advertising, the following editorial standards are enforced across all 21 pages:

1. **Elimination of Pain-Free Guarantees:** Phrases such as "100% Painless Root Canal" from legacy menus have been replaced with medically accurate terms: *"Performed under profound local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort"*.
2. **Elimination of Lifetime Guarantees:** Unconditional lifetime warranty claims on implants have been replaced with biological reality: *"Durable, long-term restorative solution when supported by routine professional periodontal maintenance"*.
3. **Transparent Procedural Boundaries:** All treatment pages state that initial bone volumes, soft tissue profiles, and individual healing responses dictate definitive treatment candidacy.
4. **Diagnostic Prerequisite Framing:** Every surgical procedure explicitly references preoperative 3D CBCT imaging and clinical diagnosis before fixture placement.
