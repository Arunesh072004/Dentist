All-on-4 Dental Implants

An established surgical protocol utilizing four strategically angled implants to support a full fixed arch of teeth, utilizing available native bone to minimize graft requirements.

Surgical Concept & Biomechanics

The All-on-4 protocol is designed specifically for patients with moderate posterior bone atrophy in the upper or lower jaw. By angling the two posterior implants up to 45 degrees, the surgeon engages higher-density anterior bone, increases the anterior-posterior (A-P) spread, and provides rigid triangulation for the full arch bridge.

This tilting mechanism often circumvents the need for complex sinus augmentation or lateral nerve transposition, significantly reducing overall surgical invasiveness and recovery intervals.

Candidate Indications

  • Completely edentulous upper or lower arches with moderate posterior bone loss
  • Patients with terminal dentition unable to undergo lengthy bone augmentation cycles
  • Individuals seeking immediate fixed transition from failing mobile bridges
  • Adequate bone density in the inter-foraminal or anterior maxillary zones

The Treatment Protocol

1. Digital Diagnostics & Virtual Surgical Guide Planning

Precise anatomical mapping evaluates bone density, nerve positions, and optimal angulation angles prior to surgical intervention.

2. Surgical Placement & Multi-Unit Abutment Connection

Four titanium fixtures are placed with precise insertion torque. Angled multi-unit abutments parallelize the prosthetic seating platform.

3. Immediate Provisional Conversion

When clinical stability thresholds are achieved, a fixed full-arch provisional prosthesis is screwed into position on the day of surgery or within 48–72 hours.

4. Osseointegration & Final Zirconia Bridge Delivery

Following 3 to 6 months of healing, the definitive computer-milled titanium-reinforced zirconia bridge is fabricated and torqued to clinical specifications.
