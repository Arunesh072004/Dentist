## 1. On-Page SEO & Indexability Standards Applied

Every public route on `delhidentalcenter.com` was audited against search discovery and indexation standards:

1. **Unique Descriptive Title Tag:** Every page features a unique `<title>` matching the clinical discipline and brand name.
2. **Accurate Meta Description:** All pages contain informative meta descriptions free of keyword stuffing.
3. **Single Semantic `<h1>`:** Strictly 1 `<h1>` tag per page defining the primary topic entity.
4. **Canonical URL Integrity:** Injected `<link rel="canonical" href="https://delhidentalcenter.com/...">` across all 21 public pages to prevent duplicate content or query string dilution.
5. **XML Sitemap (`website/sitemap.xml`):** Created a compliant sitemap containing all 21 public canonical routes with proper `changefreq` and `priority` indicators.
6. **Robots Exclusion (`website/robots.txt`):** Configured to allow all public pages, link to `sitemap.xml`, and explicitly disallow the internal QA test harness (`/preview-evidence/`).
7. **No Indexation Blockers:** Confirmed zero accidental `noindex` or `nofollow` meta tags on public clinical routes.

## 3. Dedicated Page vs. Anchor Section Decisions

### Case Study: Tooth Debonding & Bracket Removal
- **Decision:** Implemented as a comprehensive dedicated clinical section with anchor ID (`#debonding`) on [`/orthodontics/braces/#debonding`](file:///c:/Users/Administrator/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/delhi-dental-center/website/orthodontics/braces/index.html#debonding), rather than a thin standalone page.
- **Search Intent Analysis:** Patients searching for *"tooth debonding"*, *"braces removal"*, or *"debonding teeth"* seek information on procedure safety, enamel damage risks, composite glue cleanup, and retainers.
- **Content Depth:** Creating a separate `/orthodontics/debonding/` URL would generate duplicate orthodontic explanations. Hosting it within the braces treatment pathway under a dedicated anchor (`#debonding`) concentrates topic authority, preserves user context, and enables Google to index the section as a direct sitelink/fragment while providing direct on-site search navigation.
