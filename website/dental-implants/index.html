Protocol Matrix

Comparing All-on-X Surgical Approaches

Summary of clinical considerations across primary full-arch implant modalities.

Protocol Implant Count Primary Indications Bone Volume Profile
All-on-4 4 Implants per Arch Moderate posterior bone resorption; immediate loading Utilizes angled implants to avoid anatomical graft needs
All-on-6 6 Implants per Arch Patients with higher masticatory force or broader arch width Requires good posterior bone height or localized grafting
All-on-8 8 Implants per Arch Segmented prostheses with maximum individual root simulation Requires substantial bone density across the entire ridge
Zygomatic 1–4 Zygomatic Implants Extreme upper jaw bone loss; failed previous sinus grafts Bypasses deficient alveolar bone entirely into cheekbone

*Protocol suitability is determined following clinical diagnostic imaging and anatomical evaluation.
